# The mission behind Demand.io

Commerce today is chaos: fake reviews, expired codes, manipulated content. AI trained on this noise only makes it worse.

Demand.io exists to resolve the chaos. At our core is **ShopGraph**, the verified commerce knowledge graph. It powers products like **SimplyCodes** and **Product.ai**—tools used by millions to save money and make confident decisions.
